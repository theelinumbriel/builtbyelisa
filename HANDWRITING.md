# Handwritten portfolio assets

The portfolio automatically uses SVGs from `public/handwriting/` when they exist and keeps the current Helvetica labels as an accessible fallback until then.

Export these wordmarks from Freehand:

- `elisa-diop-weyer.svg`
- `portfolio.svg`
- `musings.svg`
- `about.svg`
- `contact.svg`

Freehand also exports `handwritten-alphabet.svg`, an SVG symbol sprite containing every completed uppercase and lowercase glyph.
